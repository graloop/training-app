Done as planned Done (Garmin) Planned

What you are training for, and the plan for the coming weeks. Ask Claude in Chrome to write the plan, or type it in the draft further down. Nothing changes on the calendar until you press Put this plan on the calendar.

Plan preview

Draft plan

The text the preview above is made from. Claude in Chrome writes it here, and you can edit it yourself.

Format: one session per line, written as date | sport | duration | details. Date like Tue Oct 13 or 2026-10-13. Sport: swim, bike, run or strength. Duration like 1:15 or 45 min. Details are optional (the workout itself). Two sessions on one day are two lines. Lines starting with # are comments, and rest days need no line.

About you: what Garmin doesn't know

Availability, upcoming constraints, injuries, equipment, benchmarks Garmin doesn't have. Claude reads it with the training log below.

Training log for Claude

You don't need to read this: it is what Claude reads to write your plan. What you did (from Garmin, with the effort and feel you answered on the watch), your sleep and recovery, your zones and records, your objectives and what is already planned.
